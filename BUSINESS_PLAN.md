# ReviewFlow: B2B SaaS Business Plan

## 1. Executive Summary
**ReviewFlow** is a B2B Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) designed to help local service businesses (e.g., plumbers, electricians, dentists, roofers) automatically collect, manage, and showcase video and text testimonials. By embedding a simple widget on their websites and integrating with their CRM or invoicing systems, businesses can seamlessly request reviews post-service, boosting their local SEO, building trust, and increasing conversion rates. The goal is to reach $10,000 in Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) within 12 months.

## 2. Problem Statement
Local service businesses rely heavily on word-of-mouth and online reviews to acquire new customers. However:
- Asking for reviews is awkward and often forgotten by technicians or staff.
- Customers find the process of leaving a review cumbersome.
- Video testimonials are highly converting but technically difficult for the average business to collect and display.
- Negative feedback is often aired publicly rather than resolved privately.

## 3. Solution & Product Features
**ReviewFlow** solves this by automating the review collection process via SMS and Email campaigns.
- **Automated Requests:** Triggers SMS/Email review requests automatically after a job is marked "complete" in popular CRMs (Jobber, ServiceTitan, Housecall Pro, Zapier integration).
- **Frictionless Collection:** A 1-click magic link opens a simple mobile interface where customers can record a video or type a review directly.
- **Review Gating:** 4 and 5-star reviews are pushed to Google My Business and the company's website. 1 to 3-star reviews are routed privately to the business owner for customer service resolution.
- **Website Widget:** A beautiful, embeddable "Wall of Love" widget that displays verified video and text reviews on the business's homepage.
- **AI Summary:** Auto-generates social media captions for the best reviews to share on Facebook/Instagram.

## 4. Target Market
- **Primary:** Home Service Businesses (Plumbing, HVAC, Electrical, Roofing, Landscaping) in North America.
- **Secondary:** Local Professional Services (Dentists, Chiropractors, Real Estate Agents).
- **Market Size:** There are over 5 million local service businesses in the US alone. Capturing just 100 of these at an average of $100/mo achieves the $10k MRR goal.

## 5. Competitive Analysis
| Competitor | Strengths | Weaknesses | ReviewFlow Advantage |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Birdeye / Podium** | Massive feature set, established. | Very expensive ($300-$500+/mo), bloated features, hard to set up. | Cheaper, focused entirely on reviews, simpler UI. |
| **Testimonial.to** | Great for digital creators/B2B SaaS. | Not tailored for local brick-and-mortar or SMS flows. | Built specifically for offline service businesses via SMS. |
| **Manual Follow-up** | Free. | Extremely low conversion rate, not scalable. | Automated, significantly higher conversion rate. |

## 6. Pricing Strategy (SaaS Subscription Model)
To reach $10k MRR, we need 102 customers at the Growth tier, or a mix of tiers.
- **Starter ($49/mo):** Up to 100 review requests/mo, text reviews only, basic website widget.
- **Growth ($99/mo) [Most Popular]:** Up to 500 requests/mo, Video + Text reviews, Google My Business integration, Review Gating.
- **Pro ($199/mo):** Unlimited requests, custom domain, white-labeling, dedicated account manager, API access.

## 7. Go-To-Market & Marketing Plan
**Phase 1: Validation & The First 10 Customers (Months 1-2)**
- **Cold Email / LinkedIn:** Target business owners in specific niches (e.g., HVAC in Texas). Offer a 30-day free trial or a "we'll set it up for free" concierge onboarding.
- **Facebook Groups:** Participate in niche Facebook groups (e.g., "Plumbers Business Owners") providing value about Local SEO, subtly introducing the tool.

**Phase 2: Scaling to $5k MRR (Months 3-6)**
- **Partner Program / Affiliates:** Partner with local SEO agencies and web developers. Offer them a 20% recurring commission for referring their clients to ReviewFlow.
- **Content Marketing & SEO:** Write articles comparing ReviewFlow to expensive alternatives (e.g., "Best Podium Alternatives for Small Businesses").
- **Cold Calling:** Hire a virtual assistant or do dedicated cold calling to local businesses offering to improve their Google Maps ranking through reviews.

**Phase 3: Scaling to $10k MRR (Months 7-12)**
- **Integrations Marketplaces:** Build native integrations for Jobber, ServiceTitan, and Quickbooks, and get listed on their app marketplaces.
- **Webinars & Co-marketing:** Host webinars with industry influencers on "How to double your conversion rate with video trust."

## 8. Financial Projections to $10k MRR
*Assuming an Average Revenue Per User (ARPU) of $99/mo (Growth Tier).*
- **Target Customers Needed:** ~101 customers.
- **Month 1-3:** 15 customers = $1,485 MRR
- **Month 4-6:** 40 customers = $3,960 MRR
- **Month 7-9:** 70 customers = $6,930 MRR
- **Month 10-12:** 105 customers = $10,395 MRR

## 9. Next Steps (Action Plan to Start)
1. **Week 1:** Wireframe the application and set up a landing page (Webflow/Framer) collecting waitlist emails.
2. **Week 2-4:** Build the Minimum Viable Product (MVP) using modern stack (Next.js, Supabase, Stripe, Twilio for SMS).
3. **Week 5:** Beta launch with 5 free trial users to iron out bugs.
4. **Week 6+:** Begin daily outreach (Cold Email, calls, agency partnerships) to acquire paying users.
